Creators of media content are now presented the insurmountable task of glazing to every demographic on every platform at every time. And there are algorithms that they have to please to get "recommended". One quite degenerate emergent rule of such algorithms is that **More content is good content**. The rule correlates content size favorably with content quality.

In the shadows of the back lanes, whispers of success from quantity over quality chill many, but few are held accountable when they are caught doing the same. Its the same game we have all been playing. 

And the system that has created such complexity offers a branch of peace (pronounced piss). The solution is a bunch of synthetic "monkeys" trained on a bunch of monkey text to generate more monkey text.

What's more horrifying is that unless you have already made it, (i.e. have great reputation, or connection to things that have great reputation (which is the most common)) or have connection to propaganda speaker-heads you have very little chance to get heard among the noise.

While it is quite true that people share things that they like to other people and maybe it is quite good as well. But the flame travels faster when it is fueled by a canister of lies.

And now that the synthetic monkeys have entered the game, the frothing funnel has been turned up to level we didn't know was possible. There are m x n equations for producing quantity and now there are monkeys that can make every one of them, and even suggest you more m's and n's.

Getting to understand anything on the internet is lost cause unless you find someone who you can trust who points you to places on internet that they trust. Because you wouldn't be trusting Google if it trusts Forbes as well as anything else on everything.

Where will this go, will we have something that understands what the text means and value it accordingly with an acceptable degree of error. Or are we lacking the means to get to know of things (unless we know the precise address of the thing (not even the name can be helpful))? And given the players of these games, is it even possible?
